## The Challenge

**Wohnungshelden GmbH** needed an annual ritual that accomplished multiple things at once:

- **Strategic reflection** — What did we learn this year?
- **Team alignment** — What are we doing differently next year?
- **Culture & belonging** — How do we celebrate progress and strengthen our team?

They wanted a **year-end retrospective** that wasn't just a meeting, but an event that the team looked forward to.
